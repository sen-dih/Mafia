ROLE DIRECTORY

Three sides. Too many secrets.

Roles scale with player count. A five-player match always has Assassin, Doctor, Detective, Crewmate, and one randomly selected Town role.

MAFIA

Win by eliminating the opposition.

  • AssassinKills one player each night.
  • BlackmailerSilences a player for the next day.
  • AmbusherAttacks a visitor at the watched house.
  • DisguiserBlocks one player's night ability.

TOWN

Find Mafia and keep the town alive.

  • CrewmateNo ability; discussion and voting.
  • DoctorProtects one player each night.
  • DetectiveLearns one player's role; one-round cooldown.
  • Guardian AngelProtects only after dying.
  • MayorForces an elimination; two-round cooldown.
  • DistractorBlocks one night ability, every night.

NEUTRAL

Win by your own special condition.

  • JesterWins if voted out, not if killed at night.
  • VigilanteOne bullet; Mafia hit wins alone, other hit kills them.
